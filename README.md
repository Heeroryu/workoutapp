# Training Plan — Muscle Retention Phase

**Context:** returning after a year off, at 44, in a calorie deficit on a GLP-1.
**Primary goal right now:** keep (and reclaim) muscle while the fat comes off. Maximum growth comes later, in a maintenance or slight surplus phase.

---

## The split — 4 lifting days

Upper / Lower, run twice a week. Every muscle gets trained **2x per week** instead of once. Suggested layout:

| Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Upper A | Lower A | Rest / walk | Upper B | Lower B | Rest / walk | Rest / walk |

Days can shift — just keep at least one rest day between Upper A and Upper B.

## The ramp back in

You've had a year off. Do **not** start at the set counts above.

- **Weeks 1–2:** 2 sets per exercise, stop 3–4 reps short of failure. This will feel far too easy. Do it anyway — it's the difference between training next week and being wrecked for five days.
- **Weeks 3–4:** 3 sets per exercise, stop 2–3 reps short of failure.
- **Week 5 onward:** full sets as written, last set of each exercise taken to 1–2 reps short of failure.

Strength will come back fast — most of the first 6–8 weeks is your nervous system remembering, not new tissue. Don't mistake that speed for a signal to add more volume.

---

## Progression rule

This is the part the old plan was missing entirely.

1. Log every set: weight and reps.
2. When you hit the **top of the rep range on all sets** of an exercise, add weight next session — 5 lb upper body, 10 lb lower body.
3. Reps will drop back toward the bottom of the range. Climb again. Repeat.

In a deficit, expect this to stall sooner than it would eating at maintenance. **Holding your lifts steady while losing weight is a win, not a plateau.**

---

## Cardio

- **Daily:** 7,000–10,000 steps. This is the workhorse — low fatigue cost, doesn't interfere with lifting.
- **2–3x per week:** 20–30 min easy cardio (incline treadmill, bike, elliptical) at a pace where you could still hold a conversation. Put it on non-lifting days or after lifting, not before.
- **Skip heavy HIIT for now.** It competes with lifting for the same recovery budget, and in a deficit that budget is already thin.

## Abs

Already built into Lower A and Lower B — 3 sets each day. That's enough. Visible abs are a body fat outcome, not a set-count outcome.

## What changed from the old plan

| | Old | New |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly working sets | ~145 | ~70 (ramping from ~45) |
| Frequency per muscle | 1x per week | 2x per week |
| Rest days | 2 | 3 |
| Progression scheme | none | defined |
| Redundant exercises | many | removed |
| Cardio / abs | none | included |

Dropped entirely: decline bench, front raises, machine chest press on shoulder day, behind-the-neck press, cable kickbacks, squats-to-bench, glute kickbacks, two of three calf exercises, two of four curl variations, most direct forearm work.
